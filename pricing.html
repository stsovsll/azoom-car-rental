Pricing and policies

Simple, transparent pricing. Rent longer and pay less: a week costs 6 days and a month costs 22 days.

Credit cards only. We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit cards. Debit cards, cash, PayNow and e-wallets are not accepted. The card used to reserve must be shown at pick-up.

Rental rates

All prices are in Singapore dollars and include GST. Unlimited mileage on every rental.
