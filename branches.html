How to return your car

  1. 1

    Drive to your car park

    Use the car park you chose when booking (shown in My bookings).

  2. 2

    Park in an AZoom lot

    Look for the green AZoom signs and the lot numbers listed above.

  3. 3

    Lock and drop the key

    Lock the car and drop the key card into the AZoom key box by the lift lobby.

  4. 4

    Tap "Return car"

    Confirm the return in My bookings. We'll inspect the car and send the final bill.

Return the car with at least 20% battery to avoid a S$50 recharging fee. Late returns are charged after a 30-minute grace period. See all fees.
